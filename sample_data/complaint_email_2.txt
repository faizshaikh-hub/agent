Subject: Product Complaint - Metformin HCl Tablets 850mg - Wrong Tablet Count

From: pharmacy.manager@citygeneral.hospital.com
To: complaints@pharmamanufacturer.com
Date: September 12, 2026

Dear Sir/Madam,

This is to formally report a packaging defect identified in Metformin Hydrochloride Tablets 850 mg supplied to City General Hospital, Mumbai.

Product Information:
- Product Name: Metformin HCl Tablets 850 mg
- Batch Number: MET260415
- Manufacturing Date: April 2026
- Expiry Date: March 2028
- Pack Size: 30 tablets/blister pack (3 strips of 10)

Issue Description:
During our inward quality check, we found that 5 out of 20 blister packs received contained only 28 tablets instead of the labeled 30 tablets. Two cavities in the blister strips were empty but sealed, suggesting a filling line malfunction rather than post-production tampering.

Total affected packs: 5
Total missing tablets: 10

The remaining 15 packs from the same consignment have been verified and contain the correct count.

We request:
1. Replacement of the 5 defective packs
2. Investigation report on the batch filling process
3. Confirmation on whether other shipments from this batch are affected

This has been reported to our Drug Controller as per hospital protocol.

Regards,
Dr. Priya Mehta
Chief Pharmacist
City General Hospital, Mumbai
